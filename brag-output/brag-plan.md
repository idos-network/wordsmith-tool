# Wordsmith — brag plan

**What it is:** a macOS menu bar app. Write a message in Czech, select it, tap ⌥ twice, pick one of three English versions, and it replaces your text in place. Tap ⌃ twice on any thread for a short summary in English and Czech.

**For:** anyone who thinks in another language but writes English at work (Slack, email) and wants it to sound senior, not translated. The demo uses Czech.

**Angle:** "Write in your language. Send in English." The whole video happens inside one chat box: no copy-pasting into a chatbot, the text just becomes English where you typed it.

**Hook (0–3 s):** a scrappy Czech Slack-style message types itself out under the line "Write in your language." A stranger immediately sees the setup: this is going to turn into English.

**Highlights**
1. Tap ⌥ twice → the real Wordsmith panel appears with Professional / Shorter / Casual / Keep original (real output of the app).
2. Arrow keys move the real selection, Enter → the Czech text is replaced by polished English in place.
3. Tap ⌃ twice on a long Czech thread → summary in English *and* Czech (real output).

**Punchline / outro:** app icon, "Wordsmith", "Write in your language. Send in English.", plus the two shortcuts and "runs on your Claude subscription".

**Tone:** default — punchy, playful, clean. idOS brand: near-black background with a soft aquamarine glow, key words on an aquamarine highlighter block, Urbanist, real dark-mode panel.

**Visual identity:** idOS palette (from idos.network): background #040404, greys #111111 / #1A1A1A / #A3A3A3, text #F8F8F8, accent aquamarine #00FFB9 (highlighter blocks behind key words, keystroke glow); Urbanist for captions and the outro (macOS UI stays in the system font); app icon is an aquamarine wand on a dark tile with an aquamarine outline, like the idOS logo; the real ChoicePanel rendered by the app's own code at 2×.

**Sound:** 120 BPM, D major (Dmaj7 – Bm7 – Gmaj7 – A6). Soft pad + plucked arpeggio, light kick from the reveal, hats in the highlights; effects tuned to D (key taps, panel whoosh + chime, replace sparkle) mixed under the music.
